Questions nonprofits ask us.

Is our data used to train AI models?

No. Your client, donor and program data is used only to run your assistants. It isn't used to train public models, and you can export or delete it whenever you want.

How much does it cost?

It depends on which assistants you need and how your organization works. We price for nonprofit budgets, and founding partners get special pricing. Book a call and we'll give you a clear quote.

Do we need technical staff?

No. We handle setup and connections to the tools you already use, then train your team in plain language. If you can use email, you can use these assistants.

Will AI replace our staff or volunteers?

No. The assistants handle repetitive drafting and answering, and a person approves anything important. The goal is to give your people more time for the human work only they can do.

Which tools does it work with?

Common ones like Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, spreadsheets, donor databases and website builders. Tell us what you use on the discovery call and we'll confirm.
